Workbench grid tiles now read at a glance: a tile that's **waiting for your input** glows amber with a quicker breath, a **running** tile glows green with a calm breath, and the **focused** tile gets a distinct blue selection ring and lifts instead of the barely-there white outline. Status colour, focus, and activity are now separate visual channels so they no longer mask each other.
