Review, merge, and triage without leaving Nuncio: the session's SCM tab now browses repo PRs/MRs and issues, shows review threads with reply and resolve, approves or requests changes, merges with a method picker behind a confirm dialog, and turns any issue into an agent session in one click. An Actions segment shows GitHub Actions runs and GitLab pipelines with per-job steps and log tails, plus re-run and cancel. Forge data is cached with background revalidation, so switching between SCM segments is instant.
