Run a Crew on another one of your machines, straight from Home. In hub mode the Crew composer now shows a machine picker next to the profile: pick a tailnet peer, browse a repo that lives on it, choose a Crew profile it can actually run, and start. The run executes entirely on that machine — its worktree, its writer lease, its verifier sandbox — and Home drops you onto the live view so you can follow and steer it from where you are. Solo create stays on your own machine, and single-machine setups see no change.
