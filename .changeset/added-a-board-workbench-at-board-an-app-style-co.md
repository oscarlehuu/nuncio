Added a Board workbench at /board — an app-style composer up top, a session list on the left that jumps you to any agent, and a grid of live session tiles on the right you can focus, steer inline, or maximize into the full session view. The grid adapts to how many sessions you have (a single one fills the pane) and pages when you have more than four. The standalone Tasks inbox is removed; the Board is the delegation surface now.
